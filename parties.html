Helpful Details

Group Dining FAQs

What types of occasions can I plan at Stone House?

Stone House welcomes inquiries for birthdays, anniversaries, family celebrations, corporate dinners and other group dining occasions.

Does Stone House offer party menu packages?

Yes. Three party menu packages are displayed above. Each includes appetizer, main-course and dessert selections. Contact the restaurant to confirm current pricing, selections and availability.

Does Stone House have a private room?

Available dining areas and configurations depend on the date and needs of the group. Contact our team to discuss the current options for your event.

How many guests can Stone House accommodate?

Group arrangements depend on the date, service period and dining needs. Provide your estimated headcount when you inquire so our team can discuss the available options.

Can I request outdoor seating for a group?

You may ask about seasonal patio seating when submitting your inquiry. Outdoor availability depends on the season, weather and restaurant operations.

Can I ask about dietary needs?

Yes. Include any dietary questions in your initial inquiry so the restaurant can discuss the current menu options with you. Specific accommodations should be confirmed directly with the team.

How do I begin planning?

Call Stone House at (845) 628-0598 or (845) 628-0616, email stonehousegrill1@gmail.com, or use the Contact page. Include your preferred date, estimated headcount and type of occasion.

Does an inquiry reserve my date?

No. Your event is not confirmed until you speak with the Stone House team and receive confirmation directly from the restaurant.
