Our Story

About Stone House

30 years of tradition, flavor, and community

Est. 2022

Where Every Guest
Feels at Home

Stone House Bar & Grill has been a cornerstone of the Mahopac community for over 30 years. What began as a vision to bring authentic Eastern European flavors to New York has grown into a beloved gathering place for families, friends, and neighbors.

Our founders built this restaurant on a simple promise: every guest who walks through our doors deserves to be treated like family. That philosophy has guided every decision — from the recipes we use to the staff we hire.

Today, Stone House remains a family-run establishment, and that personal touch is felt in every plate, every glass, and every smile from our team.

Our Philosophy

Crafted with Care,
Served with Pride

We believe great food starts with great ingredients. Our kitchen sources the freshest produce, premium dry-aged steaks, and seasonal seafood — because the quality of our ingredients is the foundation of everything we create.

Our culinary team brings traditional techniques passed down through generations, combined with a modern sensibility that keeps our menu exciting and relevant. Every dish is prepared from scratch, in-house, every day.

From our hand-cut steaks to our house-made sauces, there are no shortcuts at Stone House — only the same dedication to craft that has kept our guests coming back for decades.

Our Space

Dine Inside
or Under the Stars

Stone House offers a warm and inviting indoor dining room, an elegant bar area, and a beautiful outdoor patio perfect for warm-weather dining. Whether you prefer the cozy ambiance of our fireplace lounge or the fresh air of our patio, we have the perfect setting for any occasion.

Our private dining areas are available for special celebrations, business dinners, and group events. Contact us to plan your next unforgettable gathering.
