Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Stone House Bar & Grill located?

Stone House is located at 562 US-6 in Mahopac, New York 10541.

Does Stone House serve lunch and dinner?

Yes. Stone House opens at 11:30 AM and serves guests throughout the afternoon and evening. Visit our menu page to explore current lunch and dinner selections.

When is Happy Hour?

Happy Hour is Monday through Friday from 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

Can I reserve a table?

Yes. Reservations are available through OpenTable. You may also call Stone House at (845) 628-0598 or (845) 628-0616.

Does Stone House offer outdoor dining?

Seasonal outdoor dining is available on the patio. Availability depends on current conditions, so call ahead if outdoor seating is important for your visit.

Can Stone House accommodate parties and group dinners?

Stone House welcomes inquiries for birthdays, anniversaries, family celebrations, corporate dinners and other group occasions. Visit our Parties page to view party menus and contact the restaurant about available arrangements.
