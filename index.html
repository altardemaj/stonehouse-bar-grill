Our Story

Welcome to
Stone House

With over 30 years of experience and dedication, we have created a place where everyone is welcome — bringing authentic Eastern European flavors to the heart of New York.

We use only the freshest ingredients paired with traditional cooking techniques passed down through generations. Whether you're joining us for a weekday lunch or a celebratory dinner, every meal is crafted with care.

From our warm, inviting dining room to our lively bar, Stone House is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place for the community.
